# Code of Conduct

CPaste aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful of differing experience and viewpoints.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and observable behavior.
- Give and accept constructive feedback in good faith.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove personal or confidential data from examples.
- Respect licenses, attribution requirements, and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted disclosure of private information, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, code, or other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

For ordinary project concerns, open an issue. For sensitive security matters, use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/cjustsing/cpaste/security/advisories/new). Do not publish another person's private information when reporting conduct.
